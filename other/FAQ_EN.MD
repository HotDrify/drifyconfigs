<h2>❓ FAQ</h2>
<details>
  <summary><strong>How to use?</strong></summary>
  copy the link (from README) -> Download a client (see Clients) -> import the subscription -> use it!
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>Nothing works!</strong></summary>
  Update your subscription. Running it through XRay-Knife filters out 70% of non-working configs. Try the Non-Sort subscription if the checked one doesn't work.
</details>

<details>
  <summary><strong>Is it safe?</strong></summary>
  Not entirely. The server owner can easily view your traffic; if you want full security, use your own server.
</details>
